Apex GP - Basic Rules

The Race

All drivers start from a grid position determined by qualifying.
A normal Grand Prix usually lasts around 90 minutes to 2 hours.
Most races have a maximum distance of approximately 305 km.
The driver who crosses the finish line first after completing the required distance wins.

Qualifying

Qualifying decides the starting positions for the race.
Q1: All drivers compete; the slowest are eliminated.
Q2: The remaining drivers compete; more are eliminated.
Q3: The fastest drivers compete for pole position (first place on the starting grid).

Pit stops and tyres

Drivers must use different tyre compounds during a dry race.
They generally must use at least two different dry-weather tyre compounds.
Pit stops allow teams to change tyres and make repairs.
Teams cannot refuel during a race.
